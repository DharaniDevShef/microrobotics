MAGNET - 2x2 mm cyclinder - neodymium magnet
 A cylindrical neodymium magnet
(2×2 mm, N42SH grade) is embedded in the center of the
site with the N or S pole facing outward so that coupling
is induced with a stronger magnetic field along the axial
direction of the magnet.
